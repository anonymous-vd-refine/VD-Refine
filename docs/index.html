Dreaming Deeper: Stable Recurrent Medical Image Segmentation Beyond Training Rollouts

Train on short trajectories. Learn to refine beyond them.

A compact segmentation model learns from estimated late states. Eight real training steps support useful refinement beyond the training horizon—across brain MRI, liver CT and retinal images.

THE CONTRIBUTION

Train briefly.
Refine beyond the training horizon.

Longer inference exposes a recurrent model to states it rarely sees during training. Virtual-depth supervision brings those later states into a short training window.

01 / LEARNING FROM LATE STATES

Virtual depth, real supervision

Estimate a late feature state from an eight-step prefix, then supervise two real corrections. Training reaches depth 32 without executing the long prefix.

02 / A CONSISTENT STATE SCALE

Stable shared refinement

Initial-state-conditioned FiLM and multi-start training help the same Refiner operate across early and late feature states.

03 / ADJUSTABLE INFERENCE

One checkpoint, two paths

Choose a finite rollout or decode an analytic state estimate after eight real steps. Parameters stay fixed as inference depth changes.

01 / METHOD

Learn from states you do not roll out.

The Prelude encodes the image. A weight-shared Refiner updates its features. The Coda decodes the selected endpoint once.

01

Stabilize feature statistics

FiLM normalization reuses an affine transform conditioned on the initial state, giving recurrent features a common channel-moment reference.

WHY THE METHOD MATTERS

Accuracy, reliability and inference cost.

The experiments examine both segmentation quality and the behavior of refinement beyond the real training horizon.

VIRTUAL-DEPTH ABLATION

+0.53 pp

Adding virtual-depth supervision raises BraTS mean Dice at depth 32 from 84.73% to 85.26% in the paper’s matched ablation.

LONG-ROLLOUT DIAGNOSTIC

128× horizon

Accuracy stays on a stable plateau through 1024 real steps—128 times the eight-step training prefix—in the separate 500-patch BraTS diagnostic.

COMPACT PARAMETER BUDGET

4.40M

The BraTS model uses 4.40M parameters versus 31.20M for nnU-Net. The shared Refiner adds computation at inference without adding parameters.

LATE-DEPTH RELIABILITY

0.234 → 0.057 pp

Mean case-level degradation beyond decreases with virtual-depth supervision. The paired difference is −0.177 pp, with a 95% case-bootstrap interval of [−0.318, −0.062].

ACCURACY / COMPUTE

65.06 → 51.72 ms

In a matched H100 forward-timing experiment, analytic decoding reduces latency relative to , with BraTS mean Dice of 85.239% versus 85.258%.

Timing is for a four-channel input, batch size one; it is not full-volume processing time. Parameter efficiency does not imply lower latency than every baseline.

03 / CLOSER LOOK

What happens along the trajectory?

State dynamics complement segmentation scores. Long-rollout measurements are patch-level diagnostics, distinct from the full-volume benchmark.
